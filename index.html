    Get the GNS Kids App

    Put GNS Kids on your home screen so it opens like an app, full screen with its own icon. Ask a grown-up to help!

    iPhone or iPad

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    4. Tap Add. The GNS Kids icon is now on your home screen.

    Android

    1. Open this page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
    3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
    4. Tap Install. Find GNS Kids with your other apps.

    Computer

    1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
    2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
    3. Click Install. GNS Kids opens in its own window.
